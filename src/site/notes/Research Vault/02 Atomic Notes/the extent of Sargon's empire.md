“Sargon’s great expedition in the south, reaching as far as the Persian Gulf, constitutes the first phase of the empire’s expansion. During that campaign, he defeated Lugalzaggesi, king of Uruk and the other ensi ruling the cities of Sumer (Ur, E-ninmar and Umma). In his inscriptions, Sargon claimed that he had won thirty-four battles, subdued fifty ensi and washed his bloodied weapons, which now had to be repaired, in the Lower Sea. At the end of this initial phase (Figure 8.1), he already proclaimed that his rule extended from the Lower to the Upper Sea.” ([Liverani, 2014, p. 133](zotero://select/library/items/LX5B2JDD)) ([pdf](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/B4QW3W57?page=158&annotation=5Y24NA54))
